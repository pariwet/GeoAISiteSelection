# 2-hour teaching plan

## Learning outcomes
Students will be able to:
- identify spatial factors related to flood vulnerability;
- distinguish human judgement from data-driven feature importance;
- explain multi-criteria spatial decision analysis (MCDA);
- adjust weights and interpret suitability maps;
- test how changing assumptions changes candidate sites;
- question AI recommendations rather than treating them as authoritative.

## Timing
0–15 min: Mission briefing and map exploration.
15–35 min: Mission 1: rank flood factors.
35–50 min: AI-style analysis and discussion.
50–60 min: Introduce MCDA and normalization.
60–85 min: Mission 2: choose weights and run site selection.
85–105 min: Scenario challenge.
105–120 min: Group presentations.

## Suggested discussion
1. Which factor did you expect to dominate? Why?
2. Why can feature importance change with the dataset?
3. What does a high suitability score actually mean?
4. How would your decision change if roads became inaccessible?
5. What important factors are missing from the simulation?
6. Who should have the final say: AI, planner, or community?

## Assessment
- Spatial reasoning: 20%
- Criteria selection: 20%
- Weight justification: 20%
- AI questioning: 15%
- Map interpretation: 15%
- Communication: 10%
